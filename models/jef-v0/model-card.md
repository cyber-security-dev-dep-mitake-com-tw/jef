## Intended use

- **L0 research** and **L1 read-only decision support**: ranking, routing suggestions and analyst review, behind authentication.
- **Not** for unsupervised T2–T4 actions (isolate, disable, block, close or suppress). Those always need a signed grant from a person, whatever the model's confidence.
- `confidence` measures how concentrated a distribution is, not how often it is right; gate on `p_correct` only where the bucket has a validated correctness map.
